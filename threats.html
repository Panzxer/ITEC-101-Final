Common Online Threats🙀

Recognizing digital threats is the first step in defending yourself online. Below are the most common cyber threats targeting internet users today.

1. 🎣 Phishing Attacks

Phishing happens when scammers send fake emails, text messages (smishing), or social media messages pretending to be a trusted organization like a bank, school, or delivery service. Their goal is to trick you into clicking a fake link and typing in your password or PIN.

2. 👾 Malware (Malicious Software)

Malware refers to any software intentionally designed to cause damage to a computer, server, or network. Common types include:

  • Viruses: Programs that attach themselves to clean files and spread across your device.
  • Spyware: Secretly records what you type and watches your online activity.
  • Ransomware: Locks your personal files and demands money to unlock them.
  • Trojans: Harmful programs disguised as free games, pirated software, or useful apps.

3. 👤 Identity Theft & Data Breaches

When users share too much personal information on social media (such as full birthdates, home addresses, and school IDs), cybercriminals can use those details to impersonate them or guess their security questions.

4. 📶 Unsecured Public Wi-Fi

Free Wi-Fi in coffee shops, malls, or public plazas often lacks encryption. Attackers on the same network can intercept the data you send, including login passwords and messages.
