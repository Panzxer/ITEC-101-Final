Essential Digital Safety Practices😸

Practicing good digital hygiene keeps your accounts and devices safe. Focus on password security, online privacy, and safe browsing habits.

1. 🔐 Password Security & Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

  • Create passwords that are at least 12 characters long.
  • Combine uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and symbols.
  • Never reuse the same password across multiple accounts (like using your email password for gaming or social media).
  • Turn on Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA / 2FA) so logins require a one-time code sent to your phone.

2. 📊 Weak vs. Strong Password Comparison

The table below shows the difference between easily hacked passwords and secure passwords:

Security Category Weak Practice (Avoid) Strong Practice (Recommended)
Password Length Short (e.g., 123456 or admin) 12+ characters (e.g., Ocean$Basketball!Mother99)
Personal Details Using your name or birthday (e.g., andy2008) Using random unrelated words or phrases
Account Usage One password for Facebook, Email, and E-Wallet A unique password for every single website
Login Verification Password only Password + Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

3. 🌐 Safe Browsing & Online Privacy

  • Always check for https:// and the padlock icon in the browser address bar before entering sensitive details.
  • Set your social media profiles to Private or Friends Only.
  • Avoid downloading pirated movies or cracked software, as they are the most common source of malware.
