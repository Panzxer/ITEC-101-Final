✅ Step-by-Step Cyber Prevention Guide😼

If you suspect a suspicious link, email, or virus on your device, follow these ordered steps to stay protected:

  1. Stop and Pause: Do not panic when you receive an urgent message claiming your account will be closed. Scammers use urgency to make you act fast.
  2. Verify the Sender: Check the actual email address or phone number. Official companies do not use free personal email addresses.
  3. Do Not Click Links: Instead of clicking a link in an email, open your browser and type the official website address manually.
  4. Keep Software Updated: Regularly update your operating system, web browser, and antivirus software to patch security holes.
  5. Back Up Your Files: Save copies of your school projects and important documents on an external drive or cloud storage.
