Welcome to CyberShield🐱

In today's connected world, the Internet is an important tool for education, communication, and daily tasks. However, going online also exposes users to risks such as identity theft, scams, and malicious software.

This website is designed to help students and everyday internet users understand common digital threats and apply simple, effective habits to protect their personal data.

🛡️ Why Does Cyber Awareness Matter?

  • Protects Personal Privacy: Keeps your private photos, messages, and identity safe from strangers.
  • Prevents Financial Loss: Stops scammers from stealing bank details or e-wallet accounts.
  • Secures Academic & Work Files: Protects your computer and school projects from destructive viruses.
